## Reading the ratios

- `wall/process CPU/alloc ratio ≈ 1.0` → consume-side overhead negligible on that workload
- `consumer CPU p50` tracks session-owned thread ids (`jfr-metrics-consumer` + EventStream ids born after pre-start mark); 1ms sampler; pairs where owned threads exit/replace mid-sample are **invalidated and retried**
- EventStream worker identity is not a public JDK API; ownership is appearance-after-mark (best effort). Baseline measures residual owned ids after stop
- `alloc` is measured inside the workload (per-worker `getCurrentThreadAllocatedBytes`), not via live-thread snapshots
- `dropped` / `keys` are Micrometer gauges after the scenario’s last enabled leg
- Short GC Δ noise is normal; prefer wall/CPU/alloc ratios across scenarios
